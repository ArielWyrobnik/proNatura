How it works

Why Lactrase® helps

With lactose intolerance the body produces too little lactase – the enzyme that breaks milk sugar (lactose) down into its usable components glucose and galactose. Undigested lactose reaches the large intestine and causes the typical complaints there, such as bloating, abdominal pain or diarrhoea.

Lactrase® supplies highly active “acid” lactase that works already in the acidic environment of the stomach. The lactose is thus split before it enters the intestine – and milk, cheese, yoghurt or cream become digestible again.

How to use

Taking it is this simple

Choose the strength

The right FCC strength depends on the lactose content of the meal. As a guide, EFSA recommends at least 4,500 FCC per lactose-containing meal.

Take it with the meal

Take the capsule or tablet whole at the start of the lactose-containing meal with a little liquid.

Enjoy without worry

The lactase splits the milk sugar – dairy products are well tolerated again.
