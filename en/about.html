About us

Eating without worry – solutions for intolerances

As the first company in Germany, we developed and launched a lactase product as early as 1995 with Lactrase®. Lactrase® has now been the No. 1 brand in the lactase preparations segment in pharmacies for over 23 years.*

In 2006 we were the first supplier in Germany with a preparation for breaking down oligosaccharides. Three years later we brought Oligase® 600, the successor unique to Germany, into pharmacies – with a combination of four enzymes.

After more than 20 years as an expert and market leader in Germany and Austria, in 2016 we introduced Fructaid®, a product for people with the widespread intestinal fructose intolerance.

Our history

30 years of pioneering work

Since our foundation in 1994, Pro Natura has grown into a leading supplier of enzyme preparations for food intolerances – a path full of firsts.

  1. 1994

    Founded in Bad Vilbel

    Pro Natura Gesellschaft für gesunde Ernährung mbH is founded – with the aim of developing innovative products for healthy nutrition.

  2. 1995

    Lactrase® – Germany’s first lactase preparation

    As the first company in Germany, we launch a lactase product. To this day the No. 1 brand in the lactase preparations segment in pharmacies.*

  3. 2009

    Oligase® – unique enzyme blend

    Successor to Vegitase®: Germany’s first enzyme blend preparation for breaking down indigestible carbohydrates, with a combination of four enzymes.

  4. 2016

    Fructaid® – medical device for fructose intolerance

    An answer to the widespread intestinal fructose intolerance: a patented medical device for the relief and prevention of digestive complaints.

  5. 2024

    30th anniversary

    Three decades as a pioneer and market leader in pharmacies in the field of food intolerances.
