How it works

Why Oligase® 600 helps

Pulses, cabbage, onions and wholegrain products contain multiply linked sugars (oligo- and polysaccharides such as raffinose, stachyose and verbascose) that the human gut cannot break down on its own. In the large intestine they are fermented by bacteria – producing gas and bloating.

The enzyme combination in Oligase® 600 breaks these indigestible complex sugars down during digestion. This helps prevent complaints and lets you enjoy high-fibre, healthy foods again.

More enjoyment with a plant-rich diet

Beans, lentils, cabbage, broccoli or wholegrain products are valuable – but sometimes sit heavily in the stomach. Oligase® 600 supports the digestion of hard-to-digest carbohydrates, so that you can enjoy a high-fibre diet better again – at home, on the go or in a restaurant.

How to use

Taking it is this simple

Take it with the meal

Take 1–3 capsules at the start of a gas-forming meal with a little liquid – a maximum of 15 capsules per day.

The enzymes break it down

The four enzymes break down the indigestible oligo- and polysaccharides during digestion.

Prevent bloating

Less fermentation in the large intestine means less bloating – for greater well-being after eating.
