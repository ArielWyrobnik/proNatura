Our mission

Healthy nutrition for everyone

Pro Natura Gesellschaft für gesunde Ernährung mbH was founded in 1994 – with the aim of developing innovative products that enable people with food intolerances to eat healthily nonetheless.

It matters a great deal to us to offer the products at good value for money, so that as many people as possible can do something for their well-being.
