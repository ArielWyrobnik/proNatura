How it works

Why Fructaid® helps

With fructose malabsorption the small intestine can only absorb fruit sugar to a limited extent. The unabsorbed fructose reaches the large intestine and leads to bloating, abdominal pain or diarrhoea there.

Fructaid® contains the enzyme glucose isomerase, which converts part of the fructose into readily absorbable glucose while still in the small intestine. This makes fructose-containing foods such as fruit, vegetables, juices and table sugar more digestible to enjoy again.

How to use

Taking it is this simple

Take it before eating

Take 1–4 capsules shortly before a fructose-containing meal or drink with a little liquid.

The enzyme converts it

The glucose isomerase converts part of the fructose in the small intestine into well-tolerated glucose.

Prevent complaints

This helps reduce the typical complaints caused by fructose malabsorption – up to four times a day.
